Live Captions

Spoken words are continuously transcribed into readable text while the speaker talks.

Individual Translation

Common questions

Do listeners need an account? No. They simply open the room link or scan the QR code and choose a language.
Do listeners need an app? No. LiveBridge runs directly in a modern web browser.
Can every listener choose a different language? Yes. Each listener independently chooses the language they want to receive.
Can we use our own room name? Yes. During registration LiveBridge suggests a room name from your organization name, and you can customize it if the name is available.
Can I see previous broadcasts? Yes. Your account dashboard includes broadcast history and audience statistics.
How do I create a LiveBridge account? Choose a plan first, complete secure Stripe checkout, then create your organization and broadcast room from the verified registration page.
