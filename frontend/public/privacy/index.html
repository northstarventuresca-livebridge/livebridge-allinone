LiveBridge Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how North Star BAS Marketplace Ltd., operating LiveBridge, collects, uses, discloses, protects, and retains personal information in connection with the LiveBridge service.
Effective date: August 31, 2026

1. Who We Are

LiveBridge is operated by North Star BAS Marketplace Ltd. We provide live transcription, translation, translated audio, listener access, analytics, account management, and related communication tools.

2. Information We May Collect

Account and Organization Information

  • name and account-holder information;
  • organization name;
  • email address and account/login email address;
  • phone number;
  • authentication and account identifiers;
  • plan, usage, billing-status, and account-status information.

Broadcast and Content Information

  • audio submitted during an active broadcast;
  • speech transcriptions;
  • translations and translated text;
  • temporary transcript records;
  • room names and broadcast identifiers.

Listener and Usage Information

  • selected listening language;
  • listener connection/session information;
  • broadcast duration and listening time;
  • listener counts, peak listeners, and language statistics;
  • technical information reasonably necessary to operate and secure the service.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • create and administer LiveBridge accounts;
  • authenticate users and secure accounts;
  • provide live transcription, translation, and translated audio;
  • connect listeners to the correct broadcast room;
  • provide usage statistics, broadcast history, and account controls;
  • enforce plan, listener, and broadcast-time limits;
  • provide support and troubleshoot service problems;
  • detect misuse, fraud, security incidents, and technical abuse;
  • maintain and improve LiveBridge;
  • comply with legal obligations.

4. Automated Processing

LiveBridge uses automated technologies to convert speech into text, translate content, and generate spoken translated audio. Broadcast content may be sent to third-party technology providers as necessary to perform these functions.

Automated transcription and translation may be inaccurate. LiveBridge should not be treated as a guaranteed or certified human interpretation service.

5. Legal Basis, Consent and Customer Responsibilities

We collect, use, and disclose personal information only for purposes that are reasonable and connected to providing, administering, securing, and improving LiveBridge, or as otherwise permitted or required by law.

Organizations using LiveBridge are responsible for giving speakers, employees, attendees, members, guests, or other participants any notices and obtaining any consents required for the organization to transmit audio or personal information through LiveBridge.

6. Service Providers

We use third-party service providers to operate LiveBridge. Depending on the feature being used, these may include providers for:

  • authentication and account management, including Clerk;
  • hosting, networking, databases, and infrastructure, including Cloudflare;
  • artificial intelligence, transcription, translation, and language processing;
  • email, communications, and other supporting services.

These providers may process information on our behalf in accordance with their applicable agreements, privacy terms, and data-processing commitments.

7. Transcript Retention

Source-language broadcast transcript text is intended to be retained for approximately seven (7) days after a broadcast ends and then deleted automatically.

Broadcast metadata and statistics, such as dates, room identifiers, durations, listener counts, language usage, and usage totals, may be retained longer for account history, analytics, billing, support, security, and administration.

8. Audio Processing

Live broadcast audio may be processed in short segments to provide transcription and translation. Unless otherwise stated in the service, LiveBridge is not designed as a permanent raw-audio recording archive.

9. Data Location and Cross-Border Processing

LiveBridge and its service providers may process or store information in Canada, the United States, or other jurisdictions where our providers operate. As a result, information may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or loss. No internet-based system can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Account Deletion

Authorized LiveBridge administrators may delete customer accounts. Account deletion may remove the associated LiveBridge organization record, room information, broadcast history, listener analytics, administrator access, and connected authentication account, subject to legal, security, backup, and technical retention requirements.

12. Access and Correction

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request access to or correction of personal information under our control. We may need to verify identity before responding.

Account holders may also update certain profile information through LiveBridge or through an authorized LiveBridge administrator.

13. Marketing Communications

We may send service-related communications necessary to administer a LiveBridge account. Promotional or marketing communications will be sent in accordance with applicable Canadian anti-spam requirements, including consent and unsubscribe requirements where applicable.

14. Children

LiveBridge is intended for use by organizations and authorized account holders. Organizations using LiveBridge in settings involving minors are responsible for obtaining any parental, guardian, institutional, or other permissions required by law.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as LiveBridge evolves. The current version will be posted with its effective date.
